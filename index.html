Windows built for Calgary’s windy, cold climate

Calgary sits at the edge of the foothills, where many homes are exposed to strong, gusty wind. Chinook winds can change conditions within hours, temperatures regularly drop well below -20°C, and the air stays dry year-round. These swings put constant stress on seals, frames and glass, so specifications matter more here than in milder, more sheltered places.

For Calgary homes we typically recommend:

  • Triple-pane glass for better insulation through long winters
  • Low-E coatings to reduce heat loss
  • ENERGY STAR® certified products
  • Reinforced frames for wind exposure
  • Professional sealing and insulation at installation

Hail damage and window replacement in Calgary

Calgary and the surrounding region are among the most hail-prone parts of Canada. Hail can crack or shatter glass, dent and split frames and capping, tear screens, and damage seals, letting in drafts and moisture long after the storm.

We handle insurance-funded window replacements, including after hail damage.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does it cost to replace windows in Calgary?

Window replacement typically costs $700 to $1,100 per standard window, installed. Larger and custom windows cost more, and larger homes often have large openings. To get an accurate estimate, visit the replacement window costs page or book a free in-home consultation.

Which windows are best for Calgary winters and wind?

Calgary winters are long, dry and often very cold, and many homes sit on open lots. Triple-pane windows with proper sealing and insulated frames are typically recommended for improved thermal performance. Look for ENERGY STAR® certified windows. All triple-pane DraftLOCK windows are ENERGY STAR® certified.

Do you handle hail damage and insurance claims?

Yes. We handle insurance-funded window replacements, including after hail damage.

Can you replace large picture windows and custom shapes?

Yes. Our windows are manufactured to fit your openings, whether standard or not, including architectural and shaped windows. The Slim Contour Series is designed for larger openings. We also install egress windows, including enlarging existing openings.

Is it cheaper to replace all windows at once?

Yes! Replacing all of your windows at once is typically more cost-effective than replacing them one by one. The process is more efficient and the cost of doing a larger project is usually lower than the price of replacing each window individually.

How can I tell it’s time to replace my windows?

Is there a draft coming in? Have your windows started fogging up, cracking, or becoming difficult to open? These could be signs that it’s time for a replacement! Windows typically last between 15 and 20 years, but certain circumstances may warrant a sooner replacement. If you notice cracks, warps, damage or a breaking frame, or if your energy bills seem unusually high, consider reaching out to us for a free window replacement estimate. Schedule your appointment.

Is it possible to reduce my energy bill by installing windows?

Indeed, our customers have experienced up to a 30% decrease in their energy bills after window replacement! However, keep in mind that total energy savings can fluctuate between different households due to other variables like the quality of insulation and weatherproofing. Ask us about Alberta rebate and efficiency programs; eligibility depends on your property and municipality.

Is it possible to install windows during winter?

Yes. We install year-round, when weather allows, for both retrofit and full-frame installations.

Do your windows offer soundproofing capabilities?

Absolutely! Our triple-pane windows are engineered to deliver exceptional sound-reduction performance, creating a quieter and more comfortable indoor environment. For homeowners seeking the highest level of sound control, we also offer laminated glass options.

How long does a typical window replacement take?

From order to installation, most projects take 4 to 8 weeks. Most residential window replacement projects are completed within one to two days, depending on the number of windows and structural conditions. Larger projects may require additional time.

Will triple-pane windows help lower heating bills near Calgary?

Triple-pane windows are designed to reduce heat loss during long winter months. Many homeowners notice that rooms feel more consistently warm, without the thermostat working overtime. While actual savings depend on your home’s insulation and existing window condition, upgrading from older double-pane units can help improve comfort and reduce heating strain when temperatures drop well below freezing.

How do DraftLOCK windows handle Calgary’s climate?

Calgary’s climate includes dry air, strong prairie winds and rapid temperature fluctuations throughout the year. These conditions place additional demand on window insulation, sealing and frame durability. Designed for Canada’s climate, our double- and triple-pane DraftLOCK windows feature advanced sealing technology to block drafts and retain indoor warmth. All triple-pane DraftLOCK windows are ENERGY STAR® certified. It’s this level of performance that inspired the name DraftLOCK™.

Do you provide repair and service for replacement windows?

Yes, we do offer repair and service for our replacement windows on a case-by-case basis. We understand the importance of keeping your home warm, cozy, and well-insulated. While we strive to accommodate all repair requests, please note that if a significant amount of time has passed, it may be challenging to find an exact match for the parts of your existing windows. However, our dedicated team will make every effort to match the repairs to your windows as closely as possible.
